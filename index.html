07 / Questions

Good questions
deserve clear answers.

What is an AI audit?+

It is a practical review of your workflows to identify where AI or automation could reduce repetitive work. You receive prioritised recommendations, not just a list of tools.

Do I need technical knowledge?+

No. The audit is designed for business owners and small teams. We focus on your work, your goals, and what your team can realistically use.

How much time do I need to commit?+

Usually, we begin with a 15–30 minute discovery call, followed by 45–60 minute audit calls and an optional validation workshop.

What tools do you use?+

Recommendations may include low-code and no-code tools such as n8n, Make, Airtable, Claude, and Google Workspace, depending on your needs.

Is my business data safe?+

We keep the audit focused on the information needed to understand your workflows. We will discuss what should and should not be shared before any work begins.

What happens after the audit?+

You get a results walkthrough and a 30/60/90-day roadmap. If useful, we can discuss a separate paid pilot for the highest-priority opportunity.

Do you work with businesses outside Brunei?+

Yes. The audit can be delivered remotely for small businesses in Southeast Asia and beyond.
